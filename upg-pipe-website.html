About us អំពីយើង

The first ISO 9001:2015 pipe factory in the Kingdom.

UPG PIPE CO., LTD is a Cambodian manufacturing company established in June 2016. We produce and supply high-quality polymer products — uPVC pipes and fittings, electrical conduits and accessories, corrugated conduit, HDPE pipes, PPR pipes and more.

Who we are

Built here, for here.

Water pipes are a critical component of any distribution network, and leaks caused by low-quality products land squarely on the end user. We started UPG to give Cambodian projects a locally made alternative that doesn't ask anyone to compromise on that.

Today the company runs a head office in Toul Kork and a factory at Prek Phnov, with 177 people across both. Our compounds are developed in our own R&D lab, and every product family is covered by an externally audited quality management system.

Vision

To be the leading innovative and specialised manufacturer and supplier of high-quality polymer products in Cambodia and international markets.

Mission

To deliver innovative, reliable and durable polymer products that meet global standards and provide effective solutions — quality and value for every customer we serve.

How the product is proven

Five checks before it leaves the floor.

Tensile & impact

Samples pulled and struck on the bench to confirm the compound behaves the way the formulation says it should.

Hydrostatic pressure

Pipe held under sustained internal pressure in the test bath for the required duration without failure.

Dimensional check

Outside diameter, wall thickness and socket depth gauged against the published table, tolerance by tolerance.

Vertical reversion

Heat-shrink behaviour measured so the pipe stays dimensionally stable in service, not just at the die.

Visual & opacity

Surface finish, colour consistency and non-transparency inspected on every production run.

Documented & traceable

Results recorded under the ISO 9001:2015 system so a batch can be traced back if a question ever comes up.

Certification

ISO 9001:2015

With the internationally recognised Quality Management System standard ISO 9001:2015, together with the development of our own product formulations, we can hold product quality steady across every run — not just on the day of the audit.
